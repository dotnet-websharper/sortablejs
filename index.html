Plugins: MultiDrag and Swap

Sortable.Mount(MultiDrag(), Swap()) plugs in the two plugins that the sortablejs package does not mount for you. In the first list, Ctrl-click several items (or shift-click a range) and drag them together. In the second list, drop an item onto another one to swap them.

OnSpill: revert and remove

RevertOnSpill = true puts a spilled item back where it started; RemoveOnSpill = true removes it from the DOM. Both plugins are already mounted by the sortablejs entry point — drag an item out of a list and drop it on this paragraph.
